The idea

How Let Me Guess works

Tell the story. Join the call. Stop the scam.

01Score and pause

Signals and destination patterns flag risky transfers before money leaves.

02Tell the story

A personalised narrative reveals patterns a scammer cannot easily coach around.

03Join the call

At the customer’s request, the bank’s assistant questions the caller or joins a text chat.

Five-step intervention

1. Score payment risk using signals and the destination.
2. Pause high-risk transfers.
3. Story explains the likely scam pattern.
4. Answer — “Not my situation” lets you independently verify before continuing; “That’s scarily close” holds it. An unanswered high-risk prompt can be simulated.
5. AI joins the customer’s call or chat on request.

Design source: “Let Me Guess” — Scam Interrupter Westpac Hackathon Pitch, 2026. The pitch proposes a prototype with a live LLM and voice agent; this standalone HTML reproduces the user experience using local scripted simulations.
